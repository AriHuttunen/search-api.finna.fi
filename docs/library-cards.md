# Getting a library card

Each of the three systems has its own card. A card from one does not borrow
from the others.

Taken from the libraries' own pages. Helka and 3AMK were checked on 18
November 2026, Helmet and the Helka price on 19 November 2026.

## Helmet (public libraries of Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa and Kauniainen)

- One card works in all four cities. Loans can be borrowed, reserved and
  returned at any Helmet library.
- You need an address in Finland.
- Go to any Helmet library, state your address and show a valid ID. A picture
  of an ID is not enough. Accepted: EU ID cards, passports, Finnish driving
  licences, Finnish Kela cards with a photo (under 18 also without), resident
  cards from a Finnish reception centre, and residence permit cards from the
  Finnish Immigration Service.
- The card is free. Replacing a lost or damaged one costs 3 euros for adults
  and 2 euros for under-18s.
- There is a registration form to print and fill in beforehand:
  https://helmet.finna.fi/themes/custom/files/kirjastokorttihakemus_eng.pdf
- Ask for a four-digit PIN at the library when you get the card. It is needed
  for the self-service machines, renewals and reservations.
- A digital card is available in the Helmet online library and can be used
  instead of the physical one.
- Loans are normally four weeks.
- https://helmet.finna.fi/Content/kirjastokortti-ja-lainaaminen?lng=en-gb

**Children.** There is no minimum age. A child under 15 needs a guardian's
written consent and must be present when the card is made. Bring the filled-in
form and the child's Kela card or passport; a guardian without a Helmet card
brings their own ID too. The child's card is linked to the guardian's, and the
guardian is responsible for what is borrowed on it. A child's card is valid
until 18 and is then changed to an adult card at any Helmet library.
https://helmet-tukisivusto.hel.fi/en/children-and-young-people/a-library-card-for-a-child/

## Helka (University of Helsinki library)

- Open to anyone over 15 with valid ID and a street address in Finland
  (no PO box or foreign address).
- Fill in the online library card form, then collect the card with photo ID at
  the library location you chose. The personal identity code is also asked for.
- The first card is free. Replacing a lost one costs 5 euros.
- https://www.helsinki.fi/en/helsinki-university-library/library-services/library-card-and-logging-helka
- https://www.helsinki.fi/en/helsinki-university-library/welcome-library/library-everyone
- https://www.helsinki.fi/en/helsinki-university-library/visit-the-library/faq-frequently-asked-questions/faq-right-of-use-and-library-card

## 3AMK (Haaga-Helia, Laurea, Metropolia)

- You do not need to be a student at any of the three.
- Either walk into any 3AMK campus library, or fill in Metropolia's online form
  and collect the card from any 3AMK library within two working days. Bring ID.
- One card works in all three; loans can be returned to any of them.
- Some materials are lent only to the school's own students and staff.
- No price was stated on the pages read, so whether the card is free is not
  confirmed.
- https://elomake.metropolia.fi/lomakkeet/31507/lomake.html
- https://libguides.metropolia.fi/library_user/borrow
- https://libguides.haaga-helia.fi/library-services/library-card
